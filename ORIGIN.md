On **18 October 2024** GitHub created user [Nonarkara](https://github.com/Nonarkara) (`created_at` `2024-10-18T14:36:57Z`). The account was not opened to host a consultancy site, a ranking, or a satellite toolkit. It was opened to host a **city-reporter bot**.

That is the zero. Everything public in this studio is a one that grew from it.

## What "city reporter" means here, without opening the code

A reporter, in a newsroom, does two honest jobs: **gather what can be gathered today**, and **say it in a voice a resident can use**. The origin bot is that instinct applied to a city — not a vendor command center, not a ranking, not a slide.

The public studio still does those two jobs, in the open:

1. **Gather** — free, machine-readable civic feeds (water, air, satellite, transit, citizen reports, official PDFs) wired onto one axis. See [live-coding-bible](https://github.com/Nonarkara/live-coding-bible): *show different data sources on the same axis*.
2. **Say** — bilingual (and sometimes more) interfaces; AI briefs that are labelled as narrative; rankings that publish the formula; blueprints that refuse to ship a black box.

You do not need the private bot to see the descent:

| Public descendant | What it still reports |
|---|---|
| [city-hub](https://github.com/Nonarkara/city-hub) | Bangkok as a daily operating picture — air, satellite, civic alerts, a narrated brief |
| [airdash](https://github.com/Nonarkara/airdash) | Thailand PM2.5 as a 24/7 watch |
| [FloodDash-Blueprint](https://github.com/Nonarkara/FloodDash-Blueprint) + live [flood.nonarkara.org](https://flood.nonarkara.org) | Flood as a coherent picture from data that was already free |
| [nst-control-tower](https://github.com/Nonarkara/nst-control-tower) | One municipality, one question: *what is happening right now, and where do I send help?* |
| [SLIC-Index](https://github.com/Nonarkara/SLIC-Index) | Cities as an argument with evidence, not a hardship-pay table |
| [Non-Cast](https://github.com/Nonarkara/Non-Cast) | The same impulse turned into a forkable daily voice (empty at the 2026-08-31 audit) |

The reporter became a **studio**. The studio still reports.

## Why the origin stays private

A bot that talks to a city — especially on LINE — accumulates **channel credentials, subscriber identity, and operational keys**. Those are not teaching materials. The teaching materials are the later public repos that show how to correlate open data without handing over a production inbox.

If you are reconstructing the studio:

- Do **not** try to clone, scrape, or social-engineer the origin bots.
- Do **read** [PRINCIPLES.md](PRINCIPLES.md) and fork the reconstruction kit.
- Treat this page as a caption on a locked door: *this is why the door exists, and what grew after it.*

## The silent years (2024-10-18 → 2026-02-09)

The first **public** repository, [slic-landing-page](https://github.com/Nonarkara/slic-landing-page), is created on **9 February 2026**. Between account creation and that date the public listing is empty. That interval is the private workshop. This history does not fill it with invented milestones.

What we can say without invention:

- The account already existed to host a city reporter.
- By February 2026 the public work is a transparent city ranking — a reporter's move (publish the numbers, show the method) at the scale of 103 cities.
- By August 2026 the same login is a 100-plus-repo civic studio, still allergic to black-box rankings and still willing to keep a production flood system private while giving the blueprint away.

Zero to one is not a product launch. It is a reporter who kept building rooms around the desk.
